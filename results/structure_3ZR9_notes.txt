Experimental evidence:
The 3ZR9 structure was determined by X-ray diffraction at
1.91 Å resolution. The structure contains one protein chain
(Chain A), corresponding to NDM-1 from Klebsiella pneumoniae.
The deposited structure contains 231 modeled residues.

Structural interpretation:
The primary structural study reports the NDM-1 active site
in the context of other NDM-1 structures. NDM-1 belongs to the
metallo-beta-lactamase family, in which metal ions are central
to catalytic activity. Structural literature identifies
His120, His122, His189, Asp124, Cys208 and His250 as conserved
metal-binding residues of the NDM-1 active site.

Evidence boundary:
The residue-level active-site interpretation is based on the
structural literature, not inferred from the RCSB sequence
annotation interface. The RCSB deposited-coordinate record
lists ligand instances separately; therefore ligand-instance
counts are not being treated as a direct measurement of the
number of catalytically active metal ions.
